# Team Meeting Agenda

> Produced by: `tl-team-meeting-prep`

**Date:** ______________  **Length:** ______ minutes

| Time | Block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 (5m) | **Wins** | Specific and attributable. Name the person and what they did. |
| 0:05 (10m) | **Last week's actions** | Item — owner — closed or carried |
| 0:15 (20m) | **Training** | Topic: ______  Why this week: ______  Drill: ______ |
| 0:35 (10m) | **Pipeline theme** | The pattern, not individuals |
| 0:45 (10m) | **Market / product note** | `[EDUCATION]` Source: ______ Date: ______ |
| 0:55 (5m) | **Action items** | What — owner — due |

*Adjust the blocks to your actual meeting length. The minutes must sum.*

## Rules
- Individual underperformance never goes on a group agenda. That is a one-on-one.
- Every market or rate comment carries a source and a date, and is education — never a quote.
- Every meeting ends with action items that have an owner and a due date.
