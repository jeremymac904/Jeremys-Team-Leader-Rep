# Coaching prompts

## Diagnose before prescribing

> [Name], [N] months tenure. Last 4 weeks: [conversations, appointments set, appointments
> held, applications, closings]. Walk the funnel and tell me the first stage where it
> actually breaks. Tell me what this is NOT, so I stop coaching the wrong thing.

**Supply:** the full funnel, not just the closings.

---

## Two people, same number

> [Name A]: [their funnel numbers]. [Name B]: [their funnel numbers]. They both closed the
> same amount. Tell me why these are opposite problems and how I should coach each one
> differently this week.

---

## Prep the one-on-one

> Prep my one-on-one with [name]. Level: [level]. This week's scorecard: [paste]. Last
> session they committed to [what], and [did/did not] do it. Give me the number, the
> diagnosis, three questions that make them reach it themselves, one skill to drill, and
> the closing commitment.

---

## Build the development plan

> [Name] has been [below standard / ramping / plateaued] for [how long]. Numbers: [paste].
> Level: [level]. Build a 30/60/90 plan. Activity targets early, outcome targets later.
> Include what I owe them. Tell me concretely what "back on track" looks like.

---

## The accountability conversation you are avoiding

> [Name] has missed [standard] for [N] weeks. I have mentioned it [N] times informally.
> Help me have the conversation that actually changes it. Start by telling me what I should
> ask before I assume I know why.

---

## Coaching a top producer

> [Name] does [N] units/month and ignores [team process]. They are my best producer. How do
> I hold the standard without damaging the relationship — and should I even hold this
> particular standard for them? Give me the case both ways.

---

## Someone has gone quiet

> [Name] has dropped from [prior numbers] to [current] over [timeframe]. Nothing obvious
> changed in their market or their partner mix. How do I open this conversation? Assume the
> cause might not be about work.

---

## Role-play

> Role-play with me. You are a [borrower / Realtor / candidate]. Difficulty: [warm /
> neutral / difficult]. Scenario: [describe]. Stay in character until I say stop. Then score
> me on discovery, objection handling, and whether I asked for a specific next step — and
> rewrite my single weakest line with the exact words you would have used.

---

## After the session

> Turn these notes into a recap: [paste rough notes]. Structure: what we covered, what they
> committed to (one number, one focus, one next step), what I owe them, and what to check
> first next week.
