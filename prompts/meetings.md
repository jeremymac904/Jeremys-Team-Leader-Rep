# Meeting prompts

## The weekly agenda

> Build Monday's team meeting agenda, [N] minutes. Here is this week's data: [scorecards or
> pipeline review]. Last week's action items: [list, with status]. Pick the training topic
> from the actual pattern in the data, not a rotation — and tell me what pattern you saw.
> Time-box every block and make the minutes sum.

---

## The training block

> Build a [N]-minute training block on [topic] for my team. Levels range from [describe].
> At least as much drilling as teaching. Give me the exact role-play scenario and a
> one-page takeaway card they can keep.

---

## The daily huddle

> Give me a 15-minute daily huddle format for a mortgage team of [N]. It should surface
> blockers without becoming a status round-robin. Include the three questions I ask and
> what I do when someone gives a non-answer.

---

## Recap that survives

> Turn these meeting notes into a recap: [paste]. Sections: decided, assigned (owner + due
> date), carried over, next week's topic. Then draft the version I can send to the team.

---

## When the meeting is not working

> My weekly team meeting has become a status update nobody wants to attend. Here is the
> current agenda: [paste]. Diagnose why it is failing and restructure it. Be willing to tell
> me the meeting should be shorter or less frequent.

---

## Quarterly offsite or planning session

> Design a [half-day / full-day] planning session for my team of [N]. Goals for next
> quarter: [list]. I want them to leave with individual plans they actually wrote
> themselves. Give me the agenda, the exercises, and what I should NOT do.
