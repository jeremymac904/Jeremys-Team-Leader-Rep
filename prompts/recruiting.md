# Recruiting prompts

*The agent never contacts a candidate. Everything here is preparation for you.*

## Define who you are actually looking for

> I lead a [N]-person mortgage team in [market], specializing in [products]. My team
> culture is [describe]. Help me write a target candidate profile that is specific enough
> to say no with. Include what to screen OUT, which is usually the more useful half.

---

## Interview prep

> Prep me to interview [candidate]. What they say about themselves: [paste]. My target
> profile: [paste or reference]. Give me questions against the profile, what to listen for
> in the answers, and which of their claims I should verify and how.

---

## Read the numbers they gave you

> A candidate says they do [N] units/month at [company], mostly from [source]. What
> questions would tell me whether that is real and whether it travels to my team? Their
> stated numbers are unverified — treat them that way.

---

## Compare candidates

> Compare these candidates: [describe each]. Declare the criteria first, score everyone on
> the same ones, and tell me where the comparison is genuinely close. Job-related criteria
> only.

---

## The recruiting conversation

> Role-play. You are a producing LO at another company, currently [happy / frustrated /
> curious]. I am trying to earn a second conversation, not close you today. Push back
> realistically on why I should not move.

---

## Why would anyone join?

> Here is what my team actually offers: [support, leads, processing, training, culture,
> comp structure in general terms]. Write the honest version of why a producing LO would
> move here — and tell me which of these claims are weak or unprovable. Do not write
> marketing copy.

---

## First 30 days

> Build a first-30-days onboarding plan for a new LO at the [new / developing /
> established] level. Week by week: setup, training, activity targets, and their first
> checkpoint with me. Tell me what I own each week.

---

## The pipeline is empty

> I have [N] candidates in my recruiting pipeline and I need [N] hires this quarter. Where
> should I be sourcing, given a [market] mortgage team? Rank by effort-to-result, and be
> honest about which channels are slow.
