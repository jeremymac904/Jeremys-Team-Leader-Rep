# Leadership prompts

## Deciding what actually matters today

> Here is everything on my plate today: [list it, messily, in whatever order it comes out].
> I am a producing Team Leader with [N] LOs. Tell me the three things that genuinely need
> me, what to delegate and to whom, and what to just not do. Be willing to tell me
> something on this list does not matter.

**Supply:** your real list, your roster.

---

## The delegation audit

> Here is how my week actually went: [describe what you spent time on]. My team is:
> [names and roles]. What should I hand off and to whom, what should be automated, and
> what genuinely requires me? For each hand-off, tell me why that person can own it.

**Supply:** an honest account of the week, your roster with roles.

---

## Preparing a hard conversation

> I need to talk to [name] about [the specific issue]. Background: [what has happened, with
> dates and numbers]. Their level: [new/developing/established/top-producer]. Help me open
> it without putting them on the defensive, give me three questions to ask before I
> conclude anything, and tell me where this stops being coaching and becomes an HR matter.

**Supply:** the specific behavior and its history. Not "attitude problem."

---

## When you are the bottleneck

> Everything on my team seems to route through me: [describe what people come to you for].
> Which of these are real leadership decisions, which are me not having built a system, and
> which are me not trusting someone? For the second category, tell me what system to build.

---

## Setting the quarter

> Last quarter: [results]. My goals: [production, recruiting, marketing]. Team: [roster and
> levels]. Give me three objectives for next quarter, each with the number that proves it,
> and tell me what I have to stop doing to make room. Three, not seven.

---

## The reality check

> Here is my plan for [initiative]: [describe it]. Argue against it. What is the most likely
> reason this fails, what am I assuming that might not be true, and what is the cheapest way
> to find out before I commit the team to it?

**Why this one:** the agent will agree with you by default. Ask it not to.

---

## Protecting your own production

> I am a producing Team Leader. My personal goal is [N] units/month; I did [N] last month.
> Here is where my time went: [describe]. What leadership work displaced my production, and
> which of it should have been delegated, automated, or simply not done?
