# Marketing prompts

> Everything the agent produces here is a **draft**. Nothing publishes without your approval
> and, in most cases, your company's compliance review. Advertising rules apply to mortgage
> marketing in ways that are not intuitive.

---

## Content from real questions

> Here are questions my team got asked this month: [list them]. Turn the best of these into
> a content plan for [period]. Separate Realtor education from consumer education — they are
> different jobs with different compliance exposure. For each piece: hook, one point,
> concrete example, CTA, format, and who owns it.

**Why this one:** real questions produce better content than any "mortgage content ideas"
list.

---

## Video script

> Write a [N]-second video script on [topic] for [Realtors / consumers]. Hook, one point,
> concrete example, call to action. No rate or payment figures, no "guaranteed," "lowest,"
> or "approved," no competitor comparisons. Flag anything needing compliance review.

---

## Realtor education session

> Design a 30-minute Realtor education session on [topic]. What they learn, what they can
> use tomorrow, and the handout. Nothing that could read as an inducement or a rate quote —
> flag anything with RESPA exposure.

---

## Email sequence

> Write a [N]-email sequence for [past clients / dormant leads / agents]. Purpose: [what you
> want to happen]. Each email: one idea, short, useful, with a clear next step. No rates.
> Tell me which of these might need compliance review.

---

## Positioning without claims

> Help me articulate what makes my team different: [describe honestly what you actually do
> well]. Write it without any superlative, guarantee, or unprovable claim. Then tell me
> which parts are still weak or generic — I would rather hear it from you than from a
> Realtor.

---

## Content calendar at a sustainable pace

> I can realistically produce [honest capacity] per week. Build a [period] content calendar
> at exactly that pace, not more. Mix Realtor and consumer. Tell me which single piece to
> make first if I only get one done.

---

## Compliance pre-check

> Review this draft for mortgage advertising issues: [paste]. Flag rate/term/payment
> references that would trigger disclosure requirements, guarantees, superlatives,
> comparative claims, and anything that implies approval. You are flagging, not clearing —
> compliance clears.
