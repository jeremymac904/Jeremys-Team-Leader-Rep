# Follow-up cadences

Follow-up is not pestering. It is staying useful until the person is ready to move.

Most lost business is not lost to a competitor. It is lost to silence.

## Borrower and lead cadences

| Situation | Rhythm | What the message is about |
|---|---|---|
| New lead | First hour, same day, next day, then weekly until it is clear | Respond fast, find out what they are trying to do, book the consult |
| Pre-approved and looking | Weekly | Their search, updated numbers, offer readiness |
| Quoted, did not apply | Same day, 48 hours, one week | Clarify the comparison, ask what concern is still open |
| Cold lead | Monthly for 90 days, then quarterly | No-pressure check-in, has timing changed, one useful thing |
| Past client | Quarterly | Relationship first. Loan review if it helps. Referral ask when appropriate. |
| Whole database | Once per quarter, minimum | Personal note, offer to help, referral ask |

## Partner cadences

| Situation | Rhythm |
|---|---|
| New agent | Weekly until they meet you, test you, or clearly opt out |
| Warm partner | Monthly, minimum |
| A-tier partner | Weekly touch, monthly substantive value |
| Stopped referring | **Highest priority.** Find out what changed, in person if possible |

An agent who used to refer and stopped is the most valuable and most-ignored category in any
pipeline. Something happened. Usually a file went badly and nobody told you.

## What makes follow-up work

1. **Have a reason.** "Just checking in" trains people to ignore you. Bring one useful thing.
2. **Log it.** Follow-up that is not tracked is follow-up that stops after touch two.
3. **Set the next step every time.** End every touch knowing when the next one is.
4. **Retire the dead.** Some leads and some partners should be closed out. Deciding that
   consciously is not giving up; it is capacity management.

## Coaching it

Follow-up collapses first when someone gets busy — which is exactly when the pipeline needs
it most. That is why it belongs on the scorecard rather than in a periodic reminder.

The team-level version of this is [`../../automations/catalog.yaml`](../../automations/catalog.yaml):
`pipeline-preapproval-followup` and `partners-weekly-review` exist to catch the decay
systematically rather than relying on anyone remembering.
