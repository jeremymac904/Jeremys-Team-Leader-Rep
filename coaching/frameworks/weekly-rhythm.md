# The weekly rhythm

A scattered week produces scattered results. The point of a rhythm is to remove the daily
decision about what to work on — deciding is expensive and it happens at the worst possible
time, first thing in the morning.

## Theme days

Give each weekday one main job. The income block runs on that theme.

| Day | Newer / developing LOs | Experienced producers |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Database and past clients | Database reactivation + systems |
| Tuesday | Pipeline and client care | Client experience + content batching |
| Wednesday | Realtor partners | A-tier agent strategy + workflow/AI |
| Thursday | Lead conversion and follow-up | Partner production + co-marketing |
| Friday | Reviews, referrals, planning | Numbers, roadmap, people |

## The daily block

The income-producing block goes **first**, before the day turns reactive. The specific hours
matter less than the ordering.

```
Early     Win the morning     Train, read, plan, look at the number
          Prep                Pipeline, theme, call list, scripts
Morning   POWER BLOCK         Calls, conversations, follow-up, outreach   ← protect this
Midday    Appointments        Consults, agent meetings
Afternoon Pipeline & care     Updates, structuring, problem solving
Late      Admin               Email, conditions, docs, CRM
End       Close the day       Log the number, build tomorrow's list
```

Two rules make it work:

1. **Email stays out of the power block.** Email is other people's priorities.
2. **The list is built the night before.** Otherwise the first 30 minutes of the block gets
   spent deciding who to call.

## The Team Leader's own week

You are a producing LO too, which means your production is the thing that quietly
disappears. Protect it explicitly:

| Day | Leadership | Your own production |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Team meeting, week's priorities | Your power block after |
| Tuesday | Pipeline review | Your appointments |
| Wednesday | One-on-ones | Your partner meetings |
| Thursday | Training session | Your power block |
| Friday | Scorecards, weekly review | Planning + your follow-up |

If your own production is slipping, look at what leadership work displaced it — then look at
what on that list should have been delegated or automated. That is exactly what
`tl-weekly-review` and `tl-automation-advisor` are for.

## When the rhythm breaks

It will. The question is whether it breaks for a day or becomes the new normal. If every day
has become a fire day, the week has no operating system, and that is the coaching topic —
for the team and for you.
