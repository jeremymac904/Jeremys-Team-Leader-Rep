# Scripts and role-play

> Scripts are rails, not a cage. Learn the structure, then say it like a human.

Practicing a script by discussing it does not build the skill. Saying it out loud, under
mild pressure, does. That is what the [`tl-role-play`](../../.hermes/skills/team-leadership/tl-role-play/SKILL.md)
skill is for — the agent plays the other side and scores you afterward.

## The structure every script shares

1. **Open** — say who you are and why you are calling, briefly. Earn the next thirty seconds.
2. **Discover** — ask about them before you say anything about you. Most calls fail here.
3. **Position** — connect what they said to what you can do. Not a feature list.
4. **Ask** — a specific next step, with a specific time. "I'll follow up" is not an ask.

If you skip discovery, the rest of the call is guesswork.

## Practice scenarios

Run these with the role-play skill. Difficulty is warm / neutral / difficult.

**Borrower**
- First contact with an internet lead who is comparison shopping
- Buyer consultation with someone who has never bought before
- "Your rate is higher than the other guy's"
- "I want to think about it"
- "I'm already working with another lender"
- Past client reactivation after two years of silence
- Explaining why a pre-approval is conditional without scaring them

**Realtor**
- First call to an agent you have never spoken to
- "I already have a lender I use"
- The agent who tested you once, had a bad experience, and stopped
- Asking an A-tier agent for a standing weekly meeting
- A file is going sideways and you have to call the agent first

**Recruiting**
- First conversation with a producing LO at another company
- "What would I actually get here that I don't have now?"
- Someone whose stated numbers do not add up

## Rules for the practice

- **Stay in it.** Do not stop to explain what you meant. In a real call you cannot.
- **Difficult means resistant, not abusive.** Vague answers, price pressure, deflection.
- **Score against behavior, not vibe.** Did they discover before pitching? Did they ask for
  a specific next step?
- **Rewrite one line.** Ask for the exact replacement words for your weakest moment. General
  feedback like "be more consultative" changes nothing.

## What never goes in a script

- A rate or payment figure to a borrower
- "Guaranteed," "approved," "lowest," or "best"
- Any promise of an outcome
- Anything disparaging a named competitor

These are advertising and compliance issues, not style preferences. If a script drifts into
one, that is a stop, not a note.
