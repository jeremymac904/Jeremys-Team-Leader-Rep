# Brand and voice

## Positioning

A Loan Factory loan officer helps borrowers compare practical mortgage options — with
access to a broad set of programs, subject to eligibility, lender availability, and
approval.

The role to sound like is **useful advisor**. Not an influencer, not a bank, not a generic
AI content machine.

## Positioning pillars

| Pillar | What it means in content |
|---|---|
| **More options** | Multiple program paths may be available depending on borrower and property |
| **Better structure** | The right loan structure matters more than a headline rate |
| **Clear next steps** | Tell people exactly what to gather, ask, compare, or review |
| **Speed through preparation** | Clean documents and clear scenarios move faster |
| **Education before pressure** | Every post should make someone's next mortgage conversation smarter |

## Voice

Clear · practical · human · direct · confident without promising.

Written for real buyers, Realtors, investors, and past clients — not for other loan
officers.

## Approved language

> "I help clients understand their options before they make a mortgage decision."
> "My job is to help structure the file, explain the tradeoffs, and keep the next step clear."
> "If the first answer is no, I look for what may still be possible based on the full scenario."

## Strong vs weak

**Strong** — "Before you shop homes, know the payment range, cash to close, and program fit."
**Strong** — "A decline is not always the end. Sometimes the file needs a different structure."

**Weak** — lifestyle language with no mortgage lesson.
**Weak** — urgency built on unsupported rate claims.

The test: *does a reader finish this knowing something useful they did not know before?*

## Never use

- Any non-Loan Factory mortgage brand
- Individual team DBAs or prior group identities
- "Lowest rate" or "best rate" claims
- Guaranteed eligibility, universal qualification, or guaranteed closing
- "No cost" unless compliance-approved and fully disclosed
- Claims that every deal can close
- Unsupported competitor comparisons
- Misleading government-program association

## Per-Loan-Officer voice

The team shares positioning and compliance. Individual voice varies, and should.

Each Loan Officer's `brand_voice` in `config/marketing.yaml` sets tone, vocabulary level,
humour, story use, and formality. A 25-year veteran writing for investors and a new LO
writing for first-time buyers should not sound identical — forcing that produces content
neither can deliver convincingly.

What does **not** vary: the compliance guardrails and the prohibited language above.
