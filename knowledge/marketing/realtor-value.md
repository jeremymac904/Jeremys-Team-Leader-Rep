# Realtor value

## Start here

Realtors do not need another lender saying "send me your buyers."

They need clear updates, early problem detection, realistic numbers, and a loan officer who
knows how to structure a file. Content aimed at Realtors should demonstrate exactly that —
not ask for business.

## What earns the relationship

| They need | How content shows it |
|---|---|
| Fewer surprises | Name the deal risks you catch early |
| Clean pre-approvals | Show what you verify before a buyer writes |
| Fast, clear communication | Describe your update rhythm and honour it |
| Structure thinking | Work a real scenario publicly, without private detail |
| Something for their clients | Buyer education they can forward with their name on it |

## Post framework

1. **Hook** — name the deal risk
2. **Teaching point** — what causes it
3. **Value** — how you help identify options
4. **CTA** — invite a scenario review or partner conversation

Example:

> **Hook:** The buyer's pre-approval is only useful if the numbers were actually reviewed.
> **Body:** Before your client writes, I want to know the payment range, cash to close,
> program fit, and any documentation gaps. That gives you a cleaner offer strategy and
> fewer surprises later.

## Value items that are not "checking in"

- A market update they can send their own sphere
- Buyer education for their next open house
- A fast-turn commitment they can promise clients
- Co-branded content with their name on it
- Solving one problem that keeps killing their deals

Ask what actually goes wrong in their transactions. The answer is usually specific,
solvable, and not about rate.

## RESPA

**Anything of value given to a referral source has rules, and they are not intuitive.**
Marketing services agreements, co-marketing cost splits, event sponsorships, paid leads,
and gifts all carry exposure. Co-branded material is a common trip point.

Education and genuinely free content are generally safe ground.

The agent **flags** RESPA-sensitive proposals. It does not clear them. A human and
compliance decide — before the offer is made, not after.
