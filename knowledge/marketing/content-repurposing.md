# Content repurposing

Most Loan Officers do not have a content problem. They have a *volume* problem — they
create one good thing and then start from zero again.

Repurposing solves it: one substantial asset becomes a month.

## One asset, many pieces

From a single 20-minute training, webinar, podcast, or long video:

| Output | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Short video clips | 5–8 | One point each, 30–60 seconds |
| Carousel posts | 3–4 | Turn a list or process into slides |
| Written posts | 4–6 | Expand one point properly |
| Story sequences | 5–10 | Question, answer, poll |
| Email | 1–2 | The single most useful takeaway |
| Realtor one-pager | 1 | Reframed for partners |
| Blog or long post | 1 | The full transcript, cleaned up |
| FAQ entries | 3–5 | The questions asked during it |

That is 20+ pieces from one recording — several weeks at a sustainable cadence.

## Direction matters

**Long → short** is easy and should be the default. Record once, cut many.

**Short → long** works too and is underused: five related short posts that performed well
become one authoritative long piece.

**Written → video** suits LOs who write comfortably but freeze on camera — the script
already exists, so the video is just reading it.

## What repurposes best

- Anything answering a question you get repeatedly
- Explanations of something people consistently get wrong
- A process with steps
- A scenario walkthrough — no private client details
- A myth that costs people money

## What does not

- Time-sensitive market commentary — it expires
- Anything with rates or specific numbers — see
  [`marketing-compliance.md`](marketing-compliance.md)
- One-off announcements
- Content that was weak the first time. Repurposing multiplies reach, not quality.

## Rules

1. **Reframe, do not just re-cut.** A Realtor version of a buyer post needs a different
   hook and a different next step, not the same text on a new background.
2. **Re-check compliance each time.** A claim that was fine inside a full explanation can
   become misleading as a 30-second clip stripped of context. This is the most common
   repurposing failure.
3. **Space them out.** Ten pieces over three weeks, not ten in one day.
4. **Keep the disclosure with the piece.** If the original needed a footer, the clip does too.
