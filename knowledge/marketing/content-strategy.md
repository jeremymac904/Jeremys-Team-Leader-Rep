# Content strategy

The operating system behind what gets posted, and why.

## Content mix

Default balance across a month:

| Share | Pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 40% | **Buyer education** | Build trust with the people who will need a lender |
| 25% | **Scenario and problem solving** | Demonstrate competence on real files |
| 20% | **Realtor / referral partner value** | Attract and keep partners |
| 10% | **Investor and specialty program education** | Reach a higher-value niche |
| 5% | **Direct conversion** | Ask |

Only 5% is a direct ask. That ratio is the point: the other 95% is what earns the right to
make it. A feed that is mostly conversion content stops working.

Adjust per Loan Officer in `config/marketing.yaml` — a consumer-direct LO and a
Realtor-focused LO should not run the same mix.

## Cadence

A sustainable default:

- 4 posts per week
- 3–5 story sequences per week
- 1 Realtor-facing touch per week
- 1 follow-up block per week

**Match the cadence to the person, not the ideal.** An LO reliably posting twice a week
beats one who posts daily for nine days and then stops for a month. Consistency compounds;
bursts do not.

## Weekly structure

Removing the daily "what should I post?" decision is most of the battle:

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Buyer mistake, myth, or checklist |
| Tuesday | Realtor-facing scenario or deal-structure tip |
| Wednesday | Video explaining a common mortgage decision |
| Thursday | Story poll plus follow-up |
| Friday | Case-style lesson — no private client details, no guarantees |

## What every piece needs

1. **A specific audience** — not "homebuyers", but "first-time buyers who were just declined"
2. **A real mortgage problem** — something that actually costs someone money or a house
3. **One teaching point** — one, not five
4. **One practical next step**
5. **Compliance-safe language** — see [`marketing-compliance.md`](marketing-compliance.md)

If a draft is missing any of these, it is not ready.

## Quality bar

- The LO should be able to post it today with minimal editing
- Concrete mortgage context, never generic motivational content
- Short sentences and spoken language for video
- No vague sales clichés, no manufactured urgency, no generic AI phrasing
- No overused hook unless it names a specific mortgage problem

## The best content source

**The questions your team is actually being asked this week.** They are specific, current,
and provably relevant. Every content skill asks for them before falling back to general
topic generation.

Second best: a file that went sideways and what would have prevented it.
