# Video frameworks

**One mortgage problem. One lesson. One next step.** That is the whole rule.

## Structure

1. **Hook** — name a specific mortgage problem
2. **Context** — why people miss it
3. **Lesson** — the practical fix, or the question to ask
4. **CTA** — invite a scenario review or checklist
5. **Disclosure** in the caption when the content touches options, qualification, programs,
   pre-approval, application, or payment planning

## Hook patterns that work

> "Before you write an offer, check this first."
> "A pre-approval can still be weak if this was skipped."
> "If another lender said no, ask what part of the file failed."
> "Do not compare rate quotes until you understand the full cost."
> "Realtors, this is the buyer question that prevents surprises."
> "Self-employed buyers, this is where the loan conversation changes."
> "Closing costs are not one number. Here is what to separate."
> "A seller credit can help, but it has to fit the loan structure."

Every one names a **specific** problem. That is why they work. "Here are my top 3 tips"
does not.

## Reusable frameworks

| Framework | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Myth to fact** | "A lot of buyers think [myth]. The real issue is [fact]. Here is what to check." |
| **Mistake to fix** | "The mistake is [action]. The fix is [specific next step]." |
| **Realtor advisory** | "Before your buyer writes, verify [item]. It can affect [risk]." |
| **Scenario review** | "If your file has [issue], the question is not 'can I buy?' It is 'what structure fits?'" |
| **Document prep** | "The faster path starts with clean documents. Gather [items] before guessing." |

## For Loan Officers who hate being on camera

This is common and worth solving rather than pushing past. Options in rough order of
comfort:

1. **Screen recording with voiceover** — no face at all
2. **Text-on-screen with B-roll** — no face, no voice
3. **Audio-first**, repurposed to captioned clips
4. **Carousel or written post** — video is not mandatory
5. **Interview format** — being asked questions is far easier than presenting
6. **Short takes** — 20 seconds, one point, is much easier than three minutes

Do not open with "post daily video" to someone who dreads it. Start where they will
actually act, and let comfort build. An LO producing written content consistently beats one
producing nothing while feeling guilty about video.

## Never

- Vague attention hooks with no mortgage topic
- "Secret program" language
- Guaranteed eligibility or best-rate claims
- Manufactured urgency
